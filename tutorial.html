📈 Trend Benchmark

50 EMA vs 200 SMA

EMA (Exponential Moving Average) gives greater weight to recent prices, reacting faster to trend shifts. SMA (Simple Moving Average) calculates equal arithmetic weight, serving as long-term institutional support.

50-Day EMA: Institutional Pullback Level
200-Day SMA: Bull / Bear Line in the Sand
Golden Cross: 50 EMA crosses above 200 SMA
💡 Rule: Never buy a stock whose price is drifting below a falling 200-day SMA.
🌀 Mean Reversion

Fibonacci Retracement (61.8%)

Derived from the golden ratio ($1.618$). When a stock rallies strongly, pullback corrections often bounce off mathematical ratios before resuming the primary uptrend.

38.2% Retracement: Shallow / Strong Trend
50.0% Retracement: Equilibrium Benchmark
61.8% Golden Pocket: Highest Probability Bounce
💡 Rule: If price breaks below 78.6% retracement, the prior swing low is usually tested.
🏔️ Supply & Overhead

52-Week Highs & Lows

The highest and lowest closing price over 252 trading sessions. A 52-week high breakout signifies zero trapped sellers above the price, creating clear skies for expansion.

Near 52W High: Zero Overhead Resistance
At 52W Low: Heavy Trapped Supply
VCP Absorption: Contraction before Highs
💡 Psychology: Most market winners break out to fresh 52-week highs before doubling.
🛑 Capital Armor

Stop-Loss (Invalidation Level)

A stop-loss is not an emotional opinion; it is the exact price level where your original trade thesis is proven false. It must be set before entering the market.

1. Identify structural pivot support.

2. Place SL 0.5% below the pivot low.

3. Maximum loss must not exceed 1% total capital.

💡 Discipline: A 10% loss requires an 11% gain to break even. A 50% loss requires a 100% gain.
📊 Institutional Clue

Volume Footprints & VCP

Price is the advertisement; volume is the institutional budget. In a Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP), price swings tighten and volume dries up to near zero before an explosive upside breakout.

Accumulation: Tall green volume on rallies
Contraction: Volume dry-up at base lows
Distribution: Heavy volume on selloffs
💡 Detection: Breakout days must register volume at least $1.5\times$ to $2\times$ above the 50-day volume SMA.
🔄 Market Cycles

Stan Weinstein's 4 Stages

Every equity moves through 4 distinct stages. Professional capital only buys during the transition from Stage 1 into Stage 2.

Stage 1: Accumulation Base (Sideways)

Stage 2: Advancing Uptrend (BUY ZONE)

Stage 3: Distribution Top (Take Profit)

Stage 4: Capitulation Downtrend (STRICT AVOID)

💡 Focus: Align your trades so you only enter when a stock enters Stage 2.
🕯️ Trend Reversal

Bullish & Bearish Engulfing

Prior Candle
➔
Engulfing Candle
SWALLOWS

A two-candle reversal pattern where the second body completely "engulfs" the real body of the prior candle. It signals a sudden shift in market control from buyers to sellers or vice versa.

Bullish Engulfing: Green body swallows red at support
Bearish Engulfing: Red body swallows green at resistance
💡 Usage: Look for these at key structural support/resistance levels rather than mid-trend.
🔨 Rejection Wick

Hammer & Shooting Star

Hammer (Bullish)
Shooting Star

Characterized by a small real body and a long shadow (wick) that is at least $2\times$ to $3\times$ the size of the body. It represents price rejection and an aggressive intraday bounce.

Hammer: Long lower wick at market lows
Shooting Star: Long upper wick at market highs
💡 Rule: Wait for confirmation on the next candle before entering a trade off a pin bar.
➕ Equilibrium Pause

Doji (Indecision Pattern)

Standard Doji
Dragonfly Doji

Occurs when the open and close prices are virtually equal, forming a cross shape. It indicates a tug-of-war between bulls and bears where neither side could claim dominance.

Gravestone Doji: Long upper shadow (Bearish)
Dragonfly Doji: Long lower shadow (Bullish)
💡 Psychology: A Doji after a massive run tells you momentum is stalling; prepare for a reversal or consolidation.
📈 Dynamic Support

Ascending Uptrend Line

Low 1
High 1
Higher Low 2 (Bounce)

Formed by connecting at least two consecutive higher lows. It acts as an ascending diagonal floor where institutional buyers step in to defend price during pullbacks.

Validation: Requires minimum 3 touches
Action Zone: Buy / Add near the trend line touch
💡 Rule: If price breaks below a valid uptrend line on high volume, the prevailing trend has inverted to bearish.
📉 Dynamic Supply

Descending Downtrend Line

High 1
Lower High 2 (Rejection)

Formed by connecting sequential lower highs during a bear market or correction. It functions as an overhead dynamic ceiling where trapped sellers unload stock into rallies.

Behavior: Sellers aggressively active at line
Breakout Sign: Inversion to multi-month rally
💡 Psychology: A clean break above a long-term descending trend line on surging volume often triggers a massive short squeeze.
🛤️ Oscillation Range

Parallel Trend Channels

Take Profit (Top)
Buy Zone (Bottom)

Created by drawing a parallel line matching an established trend line. Price oscillates cleanly between the upper boundary (resistance/take profit) and lower boundary (support/entry).

Channel Floor: Institutional Accumulation
Channel Ceiling: Profit Booking Area
💡 Execution: Trade from boundary to boundary inside the channel until a powerful breakout occurs outside the range.
📊 Step 2: Volume

Relative Volume Spike

1.5x - 2x Avg Vol

Daily volume at least 1.5x–2x higher than average, with high delivery percentage above 50–60%.

Volume Check: > 1.5x Average
Delivery: > 50% Accumulation
Preferred Range: 1.5x to 3.0x Vol
If In Range: Institutional interest active
If Not (Low Vol): Retail noise; ignore
🔍 How to Check:
Open daily chart, look at volume bars at the bottom. Verify recent volume bar is at least 1.5x higher than average line.
💡 Execution: Heavy volume footprint proves institutional accumulation.
📈 Step 3: Structure

Consolidation Breakout

Tight Base
🚀 Breakout

Target stocks emerging after a multi-week tight base, trading cleanly above 20 EMA and 50 SMA.

Moving Averages: 20 EMA > 50 SMA
Phase: Stage 2 Uptrend
Preferred Range: 3 to 8 weeks base
If In Range: High conviction expansion
If Not (Loose): High false breakout risk
🔍 How to Check:
Add 20 EMA and 50 SMA. Confirm price built a neat tight base for 3-8 weeks and holds above rising moving averages.
💡 Execution: Enter as price breaks past resistance levels with momentum.
⚡ Step 4: Momentum

RSI & Relative Strength

RSI Sweet Zone: 55-75

Verify accelerating buying pressure with RSI(14) in the healthy 55–75 zone and outperformance vs index.

RSI Range: 55 to 75 Zone
Outperformance: Beating Index
Preferred Range: RSI 55 - 75 steady
If In Range: Optimal momentum zone
If Not (>80/<40): Overextended/dead
🔍 How to Check:
Add RSI (14) indicator. Check that current value hovers safely between 55 and 75 without hitting extreme overbought levels.
💡 Execution: Avoid overextended stocks (RSI > 80) and dead momentum stocks.
🛡️ Step 5: Risk Control

ATR & Target Setup

🎯 Target: +10% to 20%
🛑 Stop: 1.5x ATR

Use Average True Range (ATR) to place stop-losses, establishing a favorable 1:2 or 1:3 risk-to-reward ratio.

Target Objective: 10% - 20% Gains
Risk Control: Strict ATR Stop
Preferred Range: 1:2 to 1:3 R:R Ratio
If In Range: Asymmetric edge
If Not (Bad R:R): Skip trade entirely
🔍 How to Check:
Measure distance from entry to stop-loss (1.5x ATR below swing low). Verify profit target (+10% to 20%) offers 2x+ reward.
💡 Execution: Confirm no upcoming earnings or gap-risk events before entry.
🌐 Step 1: Macro First

Market Trend Filter

Index > 50 SMA
🟢 Risk-On Regime

Confirm benchmark index (e.g., Nifty/S&P 500) is trending upward above its key moving averages.

Benchmark Check: Index > 50 SMA
Market Phase: Uptrend / Bullish
Preferred Range: Index above rising 50 SMA
If In Range: Tailwinds lift breakouts
If Not (Downtrend): High fakeout risk; stay out
🔍 How to Check:
Pull up benchmark index chart with a 50 SMA. Ensure index trades above it. If falling below, stop and take no new long trades.
💡 Execution: Over 70% of stocks follow the market. Never trade against macro trends.
